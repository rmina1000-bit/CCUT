# Qwen as Primary AI Family Review

## 1. Overview
The Qwen family is identified as the **Primary AI Staff** candidate for CCUT due to its comprehensive coverage of the required modalities (ASR, LLM, VLM) and its strong performance in local execution environments.

## 2. Modality Coverage
- **Qwen3-ASR**: Specialized for audio-to-text transcription.
- **Qwen3-Instruct**: Handles Narrative Chat and generates `StoryIntentPatch` JSON.
- **Qwen-VL**: (Future) Targeted for frame understanding and vision-based analysis.
- **Qwen-Coder**: (Future) Potential candidate for automated script or code-related tasks.

## 3. Key Advantages
- **Unified Ecosystem**: ASR, LLM, and VL roles can be filled by models from the same family.
- **Local Execution**: Fully compatible with `gguf`, `llama.cpp`, and `ollama` runtimes.
- **Multilingual Support**: Strong performance in Korean and other languages.
- **JSON Compliance**: High reliability in following structured JSON instructions for `StoryIntentPatch`.
- **Specialization**: Suitable for CCUT-specific LoRA or SFT training in future stages.

## 4. Relationship with CCUT
Qwen is a **Primary AI Staff** candidate, not a permanent part of the CCUT Core. It serves the system through the AI Boundary.

## 5. Licensing Note
Actual model licenses (e.g., Apache-2.0 or custom) must be verified at the specific model repository (HuggingFace or GitHub) at the time of deployment.
